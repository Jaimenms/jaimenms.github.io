Privacy policy — bots

Last updated: September 13, 2026. Versão em português.

What it is

"bots" is the name of the Google OAuth application used by personal Telegram agents built and operated by Jaime Souza, exclusively for his own Google account. There are no other users, no sign-up and no public service.

Data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the agents read e-mail (read-only), calendar events and Google Drive files (read-only, or read-write for one agent) of Jaime Souza's account. No third-party data is collected.

How data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests on Telegram (calendar and e-mail summaries, notes, study guides). Processing happens on a private server controlled by the owner. Access tokens are stored encrypted on that server.

Sharing and retention

No data obtained from Google is sold, shared or transferred to third parties, nor used for advertising. Google Drive content is never published. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which invalidates the stored tokens.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
